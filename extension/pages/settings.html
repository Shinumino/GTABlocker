GTA Blocker settings

Words that get covered

Anything on a page that matches one of these gets covered. Capitals, accents and spacing do not matter. End a word with * to cover every word starting with it: gta* covers GTA 6 and GTA Online. Covers made by your own words say "a word from your list" on the page, so the page cannot read your list.

Sites where it is off

Turned off from the toolbar menu. Nothing is covered on these sites until you turn them back on.

Your reports

Each "Not about GTA" and each "Report missed GTA" from the toolbar menu is kept here, only on this PC (the last 50). Copy them and send them to Claude to make the covers better.
